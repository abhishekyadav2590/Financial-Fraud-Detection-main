How to interpret this

  • Counterfactuals are hypothetical model sensitivity checks, not causal conclusions.
  • Features can interact, so one change may behave differently in another transaction.
  • Changes reflect the trained model and do not guarantee real-world fraud behavior.
  • This analysis is for authorized review and education—not guidance for evading monitoring.
  • The original transaction, prediction, and review status remain unchanged.
